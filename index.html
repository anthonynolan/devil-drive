Devil Drive

Drive Mad meets Level Devil: 28 cursed levels, 1 little car, 8 colors.

How to play

  • ← → drive (throttle tilts the car!)
  • Space jump - press again mid-air to DOUBLE jump!
  • ↑ ↓ climb and dive while flying (Sky High level)
  • R restart the level
  • N skip to the next level
  • M mute the bleeps
  • X unleash your upgrades for 5 seconds (10s cooldown!)
  • 0 back to the car menu anytime
  • ← → pick your paint in the menu, 4-7 spend banked coins on upgrades (5 levels each, pricier every level!)

Reach the checkered flag at the end of each level. Grab coins and jewels for points, shields eat one hit, and stars let you splat baddies! Full throttle tips your nose down: overcook it and you'll endo! Land upside down and you explode. And if a level has the BEAST... don't stop.
